The Process Behind Every KROMA Piece

A standard print sits on top of its support. It eventually flakes, fades, or fails around moisture — because it never leaves the surface. That's not how a KROMA piece works.

An image infused, not printed

Every KROMA piece is made through thermal sublimation: ink is infused directly into the coating of the aluminium panel, not laid on top of it. The image becomes part of the panel itself. In practice, that means a surface unaffected by fingerprints, humidity, everyday cleaning products, or scratches — with no treatment or special cleaning required. The visual result gains depth too: a definition and colour relief that a surface print can't match.

Measured durability, not a marketing claim

The aluminium panel KROMA uses carries a 5+ year outdoor rating (ChromaLuxe): a durability figure established by the panel manufacturer, on vertically mounted panels under real-world conditions with direct sun exposure (tested in Florida). Confirmed resistances cover UV, humidity, abrasion, chemicals, flame, and graffiti. This isn't a KROMA commercial guarantee — it's a durability figure for the material itself, more precise than a simple marketing line.

A responsible material

The aluminium used is made from post-consumer recycled material, and remains 100% recyclable at the end of its life — consistent with a piece built to last, not to be replaced.
